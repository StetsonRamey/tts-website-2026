## Scheduling

You're on our installation list. We'll text you your invoice and installation date in November.

We are **not** able to accommodate requests for specific install dates or times. We group new clients together by neighborhood, and because each home's lighting is unique, some installs take longer than others.

## What We Ask New Customers to Provide

### ⚡ Extension cord

A 25' [extension cord](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B07B42PRY5/) to power up your lights. It can be left on the front porch, and we'll keep it with your lights for following years.

If you'd like us to provide one, we sell green extension cords for $20. Let us know before your install day and we'll add it to your invoice.

### 🏠 Soffit outlets

If your home is newer and has soffit outlets, we don't need the extension cord. On your install day, please leave your soffit outlets **switched on** so we can test your lights.

### ⏲️ Timer

A [timer](https://www.amazon.com/BN-LINK-Outdoor-Waterproof-Countdown-Grounded/dp/B07FPWK4YM/) if you want us to connect one to your lights. Home Depot and Lowe's sell them, and the link is an Amazon example.

If you have soffit outlets, you must use a **photocell timer** like the one linked. We will not install a dial timer on soffit outlets because it resets if the outlet is switched off by accident.

### 👍 Payment

Your **invoice must be paid** online before your installation date, **or** you must let us know you'll pay in person or leave payment at your home. If we don't have a paid invoice or hear from you, we will not install your lights and you will be rescheduled.

### 📞 Contact

We respond quickest to text messages sent to [(844) 600-1675](sms:8446001675). You can also call [816-281-7032](tel:8162817032) or email [hello@tts.lighting](mailto:hello@tts.lighting) and we'll respond as soon as we can.

## Fix Requests

If anything needs attention during the season, text "Fix" to [(844) 600-1675](sms:8446001675) or [create a fix request online](/create-fix-request/). In-season maintenance and repairs are included with your installation.

### 🌳 Trees and ⛳ Flags

Please trim back tree branches that touch your roofline and remove any flags within reach of your gutters before installation. Damage to bulbs, or lines becoming unclipped from trees, flags, or other objects hitting your lights, is not included in our warranty. We invoice **$50** for fixes related to trees and/or flags. You can read more in our [Terms of Service](/terms/).
