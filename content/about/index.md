## About Tis The Season Holiday Lighting

Tis the Season Holiday Lighting is a locally operated holiday lighting company based in the Lee's Summit, MO area. Since 2015, we've helped homeowners and businesses across Lee's Summit, Raymore, and surrounding communities create beautiful, hassle-free holiday displays.

![About hero placeholder](https://placehold.co/1200x500?text=About+Us)

### What We Do

We provide full-service holiday lighting — from the initial design consultation through post-season storage. That means you never have to climb a ladder, untangle a strand of lights, or figure out where to store everything when the holidays are over.

Our services include:

- Custom holiday lighting design
- Professional installation
- In-season maintenance and repair
- Post-season takedown
- Off-season storage
