# Skill: Local SEO Content

Use this skill when writing or editing homepage copy, service pages, service-area pages, FAQs, metadata, and internal linking.

## Canonical source of truth

Always use `.agents/business-facts.md` as the authoritative source for:

* company name
* service areas
* core services
* trust points
* insurance status
* years in business
* homes serviced annually
* contact details
* wording constraints

Do not invent city pages, office locations, or claims not listed there.

## SEO focus

This is a local service business website for holiday lighting.

Primary intent themes:

* holiday lighting
* holiday lighting installation
* residential holiday lighting
* commercial holiday lighting
* Christmas light installation
* Lee's Summit holiday lighting
* Raymore holiday lighting

## Content principles

Write for humans first.

Content must be:

* local
* useful
* concise
* scannable
* trust-building
* conversion-focused

Avoid:

* keyword stuffing
* repetitive city insertion
* filler paragraphs
* duplicated service-area pages
* generic AI-style marketing language

## Homepage guidance

The homepage should communicate:

* what TTS Lighting does
* who it serves
* where it serves
* why it is trustworthy
* how to request a quote

Include:

* holiday lighting as the primary service theme
* residential and commercial service
* Lee's Summit as primary location anchor
* trust points from the business facts file
* clear internal links to service-area and contact pages

## Main service page guidance

For `content/holiday-lighting/index.md`, cover:

* full-service holiday lighting
* design
* installation
* maintenance
* takedown
* storage
* residential and commercial offerings
* trust points
* FAQs
* CTA

## Service area page guidance

For each city or neighborhood page:

* localize the headline
* mention the area naturally
* explain the same core service with local relevance
* include residential and commercial applicability when appropriate
* use unique intro and FAQ framing
* include CTA

Do not create near-duplicate pages that only swap city names.

## Metadata guidance

When writing metadata:

* include service + location intent
* keep titles concise and useful
* keep meta descriptions clear and conversion-oriented

## Internal linking guidance

Link naturally between:

* homepage
* holiday-lighting page
* service area pages
* gallery
* FAQ
* contact page
* fix request page where relevant

Internal links should help visitors move toward a quote or useful next step.

## Approved trust points

Use only approved trust points from `.agents/business-facts.md`, such as:

* since 2015
* over 900 homes serviced annually
* fully insured
* lights provided
* storage included

## Accuracy constraints

Do not:

* invent an office address
* claim physical office visits
* add unsupported cities
* add commercial claims not grounded in the approved facts
* overstate market reach

## Output expectations

Every page should:

* target a clear search intent
* remain readable
* support conversion
* reflect approved business facts accurately
