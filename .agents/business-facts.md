## Holiday Lighting Specifications and Operating Details

### Estimates and Scheduling

* The contact form is the preferred estimate request method because it collects the information needed to prepare the estimate.
* Estimates include a marked-up photo of the home showing where the proposed light runs will go.
* If a customer wants lighting in another area, ask for a clear photo of that area so the markup and estimated footage can be revised.
* New-customer installations are scheduled on a first-booked, first-scheduled basis.
* New-customer scheduling begins in late October and early November, with a goal of completing installations before Thanksgiving.
* Customers receive an exact installation date well in advance after routes are built.

### Installation Scope and Attachment

* Install rooflines, garage-door outlines, door outlines, and window outlines.
* Do not advertise tree, bush, or walkway lighting; these are not currently offered.
* Use non-damaging plastic clips that attach to gutters or slide under shingle edges.
* Lighting around doors and windows generally needs to be stapled to wood trim.

### Bulbs, Colors, and Custom Designs

* C7 bulbs are 1.5 inches long and use 5 to 7 watts.
* C9 bulbs are 2.25 inches long and use 7 to 10 watts.
* All bulbs are diamond faceted, which is the standard finish.
* Warm white is 2600–2800 K.
* Pure white is 3950–4050 K.
* The standard multicolor pattern is red, yellow, green, orange, and blue; it can be customized if desired.
* Most designs are included at no additional cost. Complex custom multicolor designs may require a small first-year setup charge.
