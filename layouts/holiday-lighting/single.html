Common Questions

Holiday Lighting FAQ

How do I get an estimate?

Fill out our contact form so we have the details needed to prepare a free estimate with guaranteed first-year pricing and a marked-up design photo.

When should I schedule?

As early as possible. We begin scheduling new customers in late October and early November and aim to complete installations before Thanksgiving.

What happens if a light stops working?

Covered bulb and wire problems are repaired through our in-season maintenance service at no additional charge.

Do you remove and store the lights?

Yes. Takedown and off-season light storage are included, although wreath storage is not currently available.
