Our Story

We climb the ladders so you don't have to

Since 2015, we've been helping homeowners and businesses across Lee's Summit and Raymore create beautiful, hassle-free holiday displays. What started with a handful of homes has grown to over 900 installations a year — and we still treat every project like it's the first one.

We handle everything from the initial design consultation through post-season storage. That means you never have to climb a ladder, untangle a strand of lights, or figure out where you put the lights when it's time to hang them up again.
