Seasonal Holiday Lighting Installer

Join the Tis the Season crew for the holiday season. Great pay, a fun team, and paid training — no experience required.

$20–$25 per hour · $850–$1,100 per week · Job Open: September 22, 2026 to October 5, 2026

{{/* ── Who We Are ── */}}

Who We Are

A Local Company That Works Alongside You

We're Tis the Season Holiday Lighting — a local Lee's Summit holiday lighting company in our 8th year of business. We install lighting on over 800 homes and businesses in the Lee's Summit area each year. Our owners are heavily involved in the business and still work in the field installing lights with the crews each season.

We're looking for seasonal help with our installations and we'd love to hear from you. We pay great, we're fun to work for, and we have a great time doing what we do.

There is no experience required for this role. If you have some, that's great — but we provide all the training to get you up to speed, and you'll receive ample hands-on experience.

{{/* ── What You Will Do ── */}}

The Work

What You Will Do

  • Work with our team to install holiday lighting on our existing customers' homes and commercial businesses.
  • Work with our team to custom fit new holiday lighting for new customers' homes and businesses.
{{/* ── What You'll Need ── */}}

Requirements

What You'll Need

  • Comfort with heights.
  • Physical capability to move aluminum ladders around and ascend and descend them.
  • A healthy sense of caution and care — we work on ladders and roofs, and we must be careful doing so.
  • Ability to work well in a team environment.
  • Willingness and aptitude to pick up skills through direction and observation.
  • Comfort with physical work — you'll be working with your hands, on your feet, and up and down ladders for the majority of the day.
{{/* ── Hours & Pay ── */}}

Hours & Pay

Hours, Pay, and the Season

  • Weekly pay ranges between $850 – $1,100 per week ($20.00 – $25.00 per hour).
  • We start at 8am and are generally done by mid-afternoon (though an occasional late day happens).
  • Our season runs from now until around December 10th.
  • We generally need to work some Saturdays to keep our schedule on track.
  • Job types: Full-time, Temporary.
